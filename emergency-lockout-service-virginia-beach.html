Emergency Lockout FAQ

Answers when you are locked out of your car

I locked my keys in my car. Who should I call?

For immediate car lockout service, call a mobile locksmith with your vehicle location and make/model. Roadside assistance or auto insurance sometimes includes lockout coverage, but calling Essential Locksmith can be faster when you need direct local dispatch.

Can you help with a car lockout service?

Yes. We provide mobile car lockout service in Virginia Beach. Tell us where the vehicle is, what happened, and whether a child, pet, or safety issue makes the situation urgent.

Will roadside assistance cover a lockout?

Some plans and insurance policies do. Check your membership or policy if time allows; if you need a local estimate and an arrival window now, call us directly.

What happens before you come out?

We confirm the location and issue, discuss expected service and pricing when possible, and provide an estimated arrival window based on availability and traffic.
